Getting started

How do I add photos?

Tap any empty slot in the grid. The toggle under the grid switches between taking a photo with the camera and picking one from your library.

How do I edit a photo?

Tap a photo to open it full size. The tool bar under it has delete, save-to-library, rotate, crop, and black & white. Crop offers Original, Square, and Free aspect ratios. Tapping crop or black & white a second time undoes the edit.

How do I send?

Tap Share. Your saved recipients, subject, and message are pre-filled — edit them right in the sheet, filter down to one recipient, flip the PDF toggle to merge everything into a single attachment, then choose Email or "AirDrop, message, and more".

How do I set my default recipients?

Open Settings (the gear button) and enter email addresses separated by commas, plus your default subject and message. Save once — every future share starts from there.

Troubleshooting

The Email button says Mail isn't set up

Emailing from PhotoSendr uses Apple's Mail app. If you don't use Apple Mail, add your account in Settings → Apps → Mail → Mail Accounts, or use the "AirDrop, message, and more" option to send with Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp, and other apps.

The camera or photo picker doesn't appear

Check permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera (or Photos) and make sure PhotoSendr is allowed.

Saving to my photo library doesn't work

PhotoSendr needs "Add Photos Only" permission to save edits. Check iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PhotoSendr.

Your data

How do I delete my photos from the app?

Tap the trash button to clear all slots at once, or open a photo and use its delete button. Photos live only on your device — deleting them in the app removes them from the app's storage. Deleting the app removes everything the app stored.
