The app

PhotoSendr is a photo-sharing utility that lets you collect photos into sets, make quick edits, and send them by email or the iOS share sheet — as photo attachments or a single PDF. It's provided for your personal, lawful use.

The free tier includes one set of up to 6 photos with full editing and sending. PhotoSendr Pro adds multiple sets, sets of up to 100 items, videos, and expiring iCloud share links.

Sending is up to you — and out of our hands

PhotoSendr prepares emails and shares, but the sending happens through your own email account or Apple's system share features. Once you send something, it's in your recipients' hands — PhotoSendr can't recall it. Double-check the recipient list before you tap send, especially when using saved default recipients.

iCloud share links

Pro's share links upload the media you choose to the app's iCloud database and give you a private, unguessable web address. Anyone who has that address can view the shared media until the link expires (7 days) or you stop sharing it — so treat the link itself as carefully as the photos. While recipients can't re-share from the app, anything viewable can be saved or screenshotted; share only what you're comfortable sharing. Creating links requires an iCloud-signed-in device, and availability depends on Apple's iCloud service.
