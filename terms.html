The app

PhotoSendr is a photo-sharing utility that lets you collect photos and videos into sets, make quick edits, and send them by email or the iOS share sheet — as attachments, as a single PDF, or with Pro as an expiring iCloud link that anyone can open in a web browser. It's provided for your personal, lawful use.

The free tier includes 3 sets of up to 10 items each — photos, files, and one video up to 30 seconds per set — with full editing, document scanning, sending, and one live expiring iCloud share link at a time. PhotoSendr Pro adds unlimited sets, sets of up to 100 items, full-length videos, and share links without limits.

iCloud Backup

iCloud Backup copies your sets to your own iCloud account, using your iCloud storage and Apple's service. It is on by default, uploads over Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data, and you can turn it off or delete the copy at any time in the app. It depends on iCloud being available and on your storage not being full, so keep your own copies of anything you can't replace.

iCloud share links

Pro's share links upload the media you choose to the app's iCloud database and give you a private, unguessable web address. Anyone who has that address can view the shared media until the link expires (7 days) or you stop sharing it — so treat the link itself as carefully as the photos. While recipients can't re-share from the app, anything viewable can be saved or screenshotted; share only what you're comfortable sharing. Creating links requires an iCloud-signed-in device, and availability depends on Apple's iCloud service.
