PhotoSendr

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

PhotoSendr helps you send photos to the people who always want them — and it does all of it on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Your photos never leave your phone through us.

In short  No accounts. No tracking. No analytics. No advertising. We don't collect your photos, your recipients, or any personal information — because we have nowhere to send them.

What PhotoSendr works with

PhotoSendr works with photos you choose — taken with the camera inside the app or picked from your photo library. All editing (crop, rotate, black & white) and PDF creation happens entirely on your device using Apple's frameworks. Nothing is uploaded anywhere by the app.

What is stored on your device

  • Your photos — the photos you add to the grid are kept in the app's private storage on your device so they're still there when you come back. Deleting a photo in the app (or clearing all) removes it.
  • Your defaults — the recipient list, email subject, and message you save in Settings live on your device only.
  • Saved edits — when you tap Save, the edited photo is written back to your own photo library.

The developer has no access to any of this.

How sending works

When you share, PhotoSendr hands your photos (or the PDF it builds from them) to your own Mail compose window or the iOS share sheet. The email goes out through your email account; AirDrop and other shares go through Apple's system features. PhotoSendr never sends anything on its own, and never sees or stores what you send.

Device permissions and why

  • Camera — only to take photos inside the app, only when you choose the camera mode.
  • Photo library (selection) — only to pick the photos you choose to add.
  • Photo library (add) — only to save edited photos back when you tap Save.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, name, email, or phone number is required or collected.
  • No analytics, usage tracking, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • No location data.
  • Your photos, recipient addresses, and messages are never sent to the developer or any third-party server.

Third-party code

PhotoSendr includes one open-source library, Lottie, used solely to render the app's animations. It collects nothing and makes no network requests.

Purchases

PhotoSendr is free. There are no subscriptions, purchases, or payment processors.

Children

PhotoSendr is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes
